Process

How a new account runs

The same six steps whether it's a Shopify store or a contracting company.

01

Audit

I go through the ad accounts, pixel, store and past results to see where money is leaking and what has already worked.

02

Audience research

Who buys, why they buy and what offer moves them, built from customer behavior and sales data.

03

Strategy and creative

Campaign structure, budget split by platform and a creative testing plan with concepts and hooks for the content team.

04

Launch

Campaigns go live with clear objectives: sales, leads or customer acquisition, with tracking checked before spend starts.

05

Optimize

Daily reads on cost per purchase and ROAS. Winners get budget, losers get cut, new creatives keep testing.

06

Report

A Looker Studio or Google Sheets report that shows spend, sales and next steps in plain numbers.
